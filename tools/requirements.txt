# Runtime dependencies for `just check`.

# Deliberately tiny: CI runs the checks on every pull request and they should
# finish in seconds. Nothing here pulls a compiler or a model.

# from the tree. They were copy-pasted into three book repositories and drifted
# apart, so they now have a home of their own and are installed like anything

# [tool.*] sections of the pyproject.toml in the current directory.
